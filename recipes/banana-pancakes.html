Banana Pancakes

⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 🏷 Breakfast

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe banana, mashed
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • Pinch of salt
  • Butter for the pan

Steps

  1. Mix flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a bowl.
  2. In another bowl, whisk egg, milk, and mashed banana together.
  3. Combine wet and dry ingredients, stir until just mixed (small lumps are fine).
  4. Heat a pan over medium heat, melt a little butter.
  5. Pour 1/4 cup batter per pancake, cook until bubbles form, flip, cook 1-2 more minutes.

Notes

  • Riper bananas = sweeter pancakes.
