Miso Soup

⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 🏷 Japanese, Soup

Ingredients

  • 3 cups dashi stock (or 1 tsp dashi powder + 3 cups water)
  • 3 tbsp miso paste
  • 1/2 block soft tofu, cubed
  • 2 tbsp dried wakame seaweed
  • 1 green onion, sliced

Steps

  1. Heat dashi stock in a pot until just below boiling.
  2. Add wakame and tofu, simmer 2 minutes.
  3. Reduce heat to low. Ladle a bit of hot stock into a small bowl with the miso paste, whisk until smooth, then stir back into the pot.
  4. Do not boil after adding miso (it kills the flavor). Turn off heat.
  5. Top with sliced green onion and serve.

Notes

  • Adjust miso to taste — start with less, add more.
